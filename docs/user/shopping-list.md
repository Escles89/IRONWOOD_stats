# Recipe Calc and material coverage

Recipe Calc occupies one dashboard column when the screen supports the two-column layout. On narrow screens it uses the full width; its contents stack to fit the card.

Open **Edit** on the Status **Recipe Calc** card, or **Skills → Recipe Calc & planner → Calculator**. Choose a craftable finished item and enter a positive whole-number **Target owned quantity**. This is the total you want to own: a target of 100 with 30 already owned leaves 70 to acquire. Filter the item list by name, then select **Save & run plan**. This saves the calculator target and enables the recipe plan; automatic progression requires automation enabled and a visible tab. Use **Pause automatic plan** for manual control. See [Recipe plans](recipe-plan.md) for the run, source choices and collection controls. The list contains only outputs of current main crafting recipes, excluding raw materials, runes, obsolete recipes and failure-only outputs such as burnt food. The sole recipe is selected automatically. A recipe choice is shown only if multiple recipes become available or a saved recipe needs replacing.

The card summarizes **Target**, **Owned**, **Craftable** and **Still needed**. Craftable is additional output supported by observed materials, excluding finished items already owned. Still needed is the remaining target gap after owned items and crafting capacity; uncertain capacity stays labelled. The coverage bar separates owned and producible output.

The card shows your target, material shortages and a compact centered recipe tree, with the target above its ingredients. Each square icon is an item or resource using the game's artwork. Hover for its name and supply status, or click it to open a detail window. The window shows the chosen recipe, required and owned quantities, stock used, attempts, projected surplus and any missing information. Input totals combine shared requirements across the plan; projected surplus is not owned inventory.

Owned intermediates reduce the work before their recipes expand. When an intermediate has multiple current recipes, choose one in its detail window. Your choices remain saved when you edit the target quantity. Missing or invalidated choices are flagged by warning icons and explained in the detail window; they are never silently replaced. Verified noncraftable items are listed to acquire, without performing an acquisition. The linked recipe plan can select gathering sources; purchases and combat drops remain manual. Cycles stop their affected branch with an explanation; other known requirements remain visible.

The plan uses base costs and owned stock only, all available to this single plan without reservations. Pending loot, unfinished queues, preservation savings and bonus output do not count. Recipes with variable or failure-prone output are marked uncertain. That uncertainty also applies to every parent that needs the intermediate output. Nominal totals are a planning basis, not a promise of the finished quantity. Unknown totals retain a labelled known subtotal where possible.

**Target satisfied** means the finished items are already owned. **Materials covered** means the observed full-chain inputs cover a supported base recipe calculation; it does not mean crafting has occurred. Unknown data remains unknown.

Use **Edit** on Recipe Calc to open the combined editor. **Inputs** and **Clear target** are in Calculator; **Refresh plan** in Active plan checks inventory. Cancelling edits preserves the saved target. Clearing the active plan and clearing the calculator target are separate controls. The target and recipe choices are saved separately for each character and game mode in this browser. Satisfied targets stay saved and show a shortfall again after spending items. If a saved recipe disappears, choose a current one explicitly. Refresh reads the game state without starting crafting or collecting loot; an unavailable refresh retains the last observation and its age. Native recipe links open the ordinary game page without starting an action.

Use Tab to move between item icons and Enter or Space to open details. Escape, the close button or a click outside closes the window and returns focus to the icon. Within the window, Tab stays among its controls. On narrow screens, detail quantities appear in labelled two-column rows. Opening details does not start crafting; the window updates as observations change.

A red icon with **!** means a known material shortage in that branch; the warning also appears on every parent that depends on it. Amber **?** marks unknown data or uncertain output. Green connections mean owned coverage; blue connections mean production is needed but materials cover it. Diamonds use the same supply colors. Covered branches stop expanding. These describe the supply plan, not completed crafting. **Inputs** can select Potion Mix, Metal Parts, Charcoal and Arcane Powder sources. Automatic wood/gem choices allocate spare owned stock after other requirements; resource-balance-only choices exclude conversion inputs. Conversions remain manual inventory actions. Shared ingredient icons open the same combined detail, so stock is counted only once.
