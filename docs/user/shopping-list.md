# Shopping list

At the bottom of Status, choose a craftable finished item and enter a positive whole-number **Target owned quantity**. This is the total you want to own: a target of 100 with 30 already owned leaves 70 to acquire. Filter the item list by name, then save. The list contains only outputs of current main crafting recipes, excluding raw materials, runes, obsolete recipes and failure-only outputs such as burnt food. The sole recipe is selected automatically. A recipe choice is shown only if multiple recipes become available or a saved recipe needs replacing.

The card shows a compact summary of materials to acquire, including separate native resource balances such as Charcoal. **Full recipe chain** shows a connected tree with item icons; collapse it when you only need the compact shortage summary. Click any item to see its chosen recipe, stock used, remaining output to produce, attempts and projected surplus. Inputs show per-attempt costs and required quantities; their Owned and Missing columns refer to the combined plan totals. Shared ingredients appear once as production steps, combining every parent's demand before using stock and rounding attempts. Projected surplus is not owned inventory.

Owned intermediates reduce the work before their recipes expand. When an intermediate has multiple current recipes, choose one in its expanded step. Your choices remain saved when you edit the target quantity. Missing or invalidated choices appear under **Unresolved branches / uncertain production**; they are never silently replaced. Verified noncraftable items are listed to acquire, without choosing between gathering and buying. Cycles stop their affected branch with an explanation; other known requirements remain visible.

The plan uses base costs and owned stock only, all available to this single plan without reservations. Pending loot, unfinished queues, preservation savings and bonus output do not count. Recipes with variable or failure-prone output are marked uncertain. That uncertainty also applies to every parent that needs the intermediate output. Nominal totals are a planning basis, not a promise of the finished quantity. Unknown totals retain a labelled known subtotal where possible.

**Target satisfied** means the finished items are already owned. **Base materials covered** means the observed full-chain inputs cover a supported base recipe calculation; it does not mean crafting has occurred. Unknown data remains unknown.

Use **Edit**, **Clear** or **Refresh** as needed. The target and recipe choices are saved separately for each character and game mode in this browser. Satisfied targets stay saved and show a shortfall again after spending items. If a saved recipe disappears, choose a current one explicitly. Refresh reads the game state without starting crafting or collecting loot; an unavailable refresh retains the last observation and its age. Native recipe links open the ordinary game page without starting an action.

Use Tab to move between controls and native recipe links, Enter to save the target or expand a recipe, and the ordinary keyboard controls for recipe dropdowns. On narrow screens, quantities appear in labelled two-column rows. Expanding a step does not start crafting; expanded steps remain open while observations update.

A red icon with **!** means a known material shortage in that branch; the warning also appears on every parent that depends on it. Amber **?** marks unknown data or uncertain output, and green **✓** means the branch's base materials are covered. These describe the supply plan, not completed crafting. Shared ingredients link to their combined step so stock is still counted only once. The tree stays visible while you open an individual step's details.
